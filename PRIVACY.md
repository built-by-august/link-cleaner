# Privacy Policy — Link Cleaner Pro

**Last updated:** July 3, 2026

## What data does Link Cleaner collect?

**None.**

Link Cleaner Pro does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, browsing history, or URL content.

## How does the extension work?

All processing happens **entirely on your device** — in your local browser. When you:
- **Paste a URL** into the popup tool — it's cleaned locally in JavaScript
- **Copy a URL** from any page — the content script intercepts the copy event and strips tracking parameters before the URL reaches your clipboard
- **Right-click a link** — the right-click menu copies the cleaned URL to your clipboard

No data ever leaves your machine.

## What about the Stripe purchase flow?

When you upgrade to Pro via Stripe, your payment is processed entirely by **Stripe** — not by Link Cleaner. We receive no credit card details or personal payment information. Stripe's privacy policy applies to that transaction.

## Does the extension use any analytics or tracking?

**No.** There are no analytics SDKs, no telemetry, no crash reporters, no cookies, and no network requests from the extension itself.

## Children's privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
